# RoutineCoach

A lightweight habit-tracking tool for people who have many ideas but rarely follow through on them — built specifically for young adults with ADHD navigating everyday life.

## What it does

At the start of each week, you define three observable behavioral goals. Small, concrete, measurable ones. Not "exercise more" but "I walked for 15 minutes before school."

Every day the app asks one question: **Did I work toward my goal today? Yes or No.**

That's it. At the end of the week you see how often you actually followed through — and you decide whether to continue into the next week or adjust your approach.

Push notifications remind you once or twice a day to stay on track. No scoring system, no streaks, no gamification. Just honest self-observation.

## Why it exists

I work as a school psychologist and ADHD coach in Swiss vocational education. I see the same pattern constantly: young adults with real potential who can't bridge the gap between intention and action. Not because they don't want to, but because their nervous system keeps getting in the way.

Most habit apps are built for neurotypical users. They reward consistency, punish missed days, and create pressure instead of reducing it. RoutineCoach is built around a different assumption: that awareness alone creates movement, and that small observable steps matter more than perfect streaks.
